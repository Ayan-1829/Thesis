Thesis

How to do research in computer science, from the first idea to the final defence. Part A walks through the research process: choosing a topic, finding and reading papers, designing your own methodology, running experiments, writing and defending the thesis. Part B introduces ten research fields with their problems, datasets, methods, venues and thesis ideas.
