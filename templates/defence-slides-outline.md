# Defence talk outline (15–20 minutes, about 12 slides)

1. Title — thesis title, names, supervisor, date
2. The problem — one picture or number that shows why it matters
3. The gap — what existing work does not do (two or three key papers)
4. Research question(s) and contributions
5. Data — source, size, labelling, split
6. Method — one diagram of the pipeline or system
7. Experimental setup — baselines, metrics, runs, statistics
8. Main result — one table or chart, the key number highlighted, with its confidence interval
9. Why it works — ablation
10. Where it fails — error analysis with real examples
11. Limitations and future work
12. Conclusion — answer each research question in one sentence

Backup slides: extra results, hyperparameters, more error examples, answers to likely questions.
