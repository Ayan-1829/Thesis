# Interview guide — <study title>

Research question:
Planned length: 30–45 minutes · Pilot with: <name> on <date>

## Before the interview
- Information sheet given; consent form signed; permission to record.
- "You can skip any question or stop at any time. There are no right answers."

## Warm-up (5 min)
1. Could you tell me a little about your study or work?

## Main questions (25–30 min) — open, one idea each
2. Tell me about the last time you <did the activity>.
3. How do you usually <…>?
4. What happened the last time <…> did not work?
5. What, if anything, would you change about <…>?

## Probes (use any time)
- Can you give me an example?
- What happened next?
- You said "<their words>". What does that look like?
- How did you feel about that?

## Close (5 min)
- Is there anything I should have asked but did not?
- Thank the participant; explain what happens to the data.
