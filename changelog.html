Version 1.1 · 26 November 2026

  • Chapter 8: correct definition of the p-value; choosing a test by what one pair is (seeds, cross-validation folds, test sets); the corrected resampled t-test for folds; why Wilcoxon needs at least 6 pairs; effect sizes, confidence intervals and multiple comparisons; ablation table with mean ± sd; a new worked example on cross-validation.
  • Significance-test calculator: a typo is reported instead of silently skipped, unequal lists are refused, zero-variance differences are explained instead of declared significant; new cross-validation mode, 95% confidence interval and exact Wilcoxon p-value.
  • Chapter 2: the four-part question template is now for comparative studies, with an exploratory example beside it. Chapter 13: the practice answer on AI areas outside machine learning is corrected.
  • New chapters: 22 Computer Architecture and Digital Hardware Design (the hardware design flow and a cache-size experiment), 23 Qualitative and Mixed Methods, 24 Managing Your Thesis Year.
  • New pages: a worked thesis followed from idea to defence, a glossary and a site search. Downloadable templates on the milestones page.
  • Every Part A chapter ends with a task to bring to your supervisor, with an acceptable example.
  • Slides are labelled “Section 2 of 6” to match the chapter outline. The home page shows your progress and the time each chapter takes. Without JavaScript the slides are shown one after another.
