# Thesis — an interactive guide to undergraduate research in computer science

**Live site: https://ayan-1829.github.io/Thesis/**

Part A (chapters 1–11) teaches the research process: topic and research questions, searching, selecting and reading papers,
the literature review, methodology, experiments and statistics, ethics, writing, defence and publishing.
Part B (chapters 12–21) introduces ten research fields. Every chapter is a slide deck with figures, interactive tools,
worked examples, practice, a quiz and resources.
