LaTeX thesis template

A starting point with the usual front matter and chapters. Upload it to Overleaf (New project → Upload), add references.bib and a figures/ folder, and follow your department’s formatting rules where they differ.

Download .tex
\documentclass[12pt,a4paper,oneside]{report}
\usepackage[utf8]{inputenc}
\usepackage[margin=1in]{geometry}
\usepackage{setspace}\onehalfspacing
\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb,graphicx,booktabs,caption,subcaption,float}
\usepackage[ruled,vlined]{algorithm2e}
\usepackage[hidelinks]{hyperref}
\usepackage[style=ieee,backend=biber]{biblatex}
\addbibresource{references.bib}

\begin{document}
% ---------- front matter ----------
\begin{titlepage}
  \centering
  {\Large Department of Computer Science and Engineering\par}\vspace{2cm}
  {\LARGE\bfseries Title of Your Thesis\par}\vspace{1cm}
  {\large A thesis submitted in partial fulfilment of the requirements\\for the degree of B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering\par}\vspace{2cm}
  {\large Author Name (ID)\\Author Name (ID)\par}\vspace{1cm}
  {\large Supervised by\\Supervisor Name, Designation\par}\vfill
  {\large Month Year\par}
\end{titlepage}

\pagenumbering{roman}
\chapter*{Declaration}      % original work, sources acknowledged; signed by every author
\chapter*{Approval}         % supervisor and examiners
\chapter*{Acknowledgements}
\chapter*{Abstract}         % 150–300 words: context, problem, method, results, conclusion
\tableofcontents
\listoffigures
\listoftables

% ---------- main matter ----------
\clearpage\pagenumbering{arabic}
\chapter{Introduction}      % background, problem, motivation, research questions, contributions, outline
\chapter{Literature Review} % themes, comparison table, the gap
\chapter{Methodology}       % data, proposed method, baselines, metrics, experimental setup
\chapter{Results and Discussion}
\chapter{Conclusion and Future Work}

\printbibliography[heading=bibintoc]
\appendix
\chapter{Additional Results}
\end{document}
