The research journey

Eight stages take you from a first idea to a defended thesis. Each one links to the chapters that teach it.

Your progress (slides read, best quiz score) is saved in this browser and shown on each chapter.

  1. Chapter 2Choose a topicNarrow an area of interest to one research question.
  2. Chapters 3–5Search and readFind the papers that matter, judge them and take notes.
  3. Chapter 6Review the literatureCompare what is known and state the gap you will fill.
  4. Chapter 7Design the methodFix the design, baselines and metrics before you start.
  5. Chapter 9Plan the ethicsConsent, privacy and data use, settled before you collect any data.
  6. Chapter 8Run the experimentsFair splits, repeated runs and a test of whether differences are real.
  7. Chapter 10Write the thesisWrite each chapter honestly, with every source cited.Reporting your ethics decisions and AI use: Chapter 9 →
  8. Chapter 11Defend and publishPresent the work, answer the examiners and share it.
